WHAT WE DO

We do not decorate spaces; we engineer structural narratives. We operate at the intersection of absolute spatial utility and raw material texture.

Established in 2015 under the technical leadership of Director Karthikeyan Viswanathan, Archintenz transcends traditional architectural service models. We govern the entire lifecycle of the build—from the initial geometric parameter and site feasibility, to high-fidelity digital prototyping and final turnkey execution. We design spaces to function as permanent physical legacies.

The founding partners of the studio actively participate in each project. The project Leader coordinates the entire process, ensuring compliance with objectives, deadlines, budget and quality. The creative and interior design team translates the client needs in unique concepts and spaces, while the Project Manager and the construction manager ensures the technical development, execution and control of times and costs. In addition, we collaborate with a network of external specialists, from engineers and landscapers to designers and artists, to offer solutions for each project.
